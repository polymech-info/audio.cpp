In a gymnasium, a spiky brownish-gray thought shape slides across the polished wooden floor on a burst of yellow. A young boy forcefully casts it aside. Wooden stall bars, a blue mat, and hanging gymnastics rings surround him. The boy has curly red hair, green eyes, freckles, and a determined expression. He wears a dark blue wrestling singlet with red stripes and blue high-top shoes. He balances on his right foot, kicks his left leg back, leans forward, and reaches toward the floor. The words on the thought shape say, “Nobody loves you so run away.”
